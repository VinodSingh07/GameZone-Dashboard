# 🎮 Sales Performance Dashboard (Product) | GameZone

An interactive **Tableau Sales Performance Dashboard** built to analyze GameZone's product sales performance across different regions, marketing channels, and time periods. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales trends, evaluate product performance, analyze refund patterns, and make data-driven business decisions through dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

# 📌 Short Description

The **GameZone Sales Performance Dashboard** provides a comprehensive overview of product sales by combining key business metrics with interactive visualizations. It helps business users identify top-performing products, monitor Average Order Value (AOV), track refunds, compare regional sales performance, and evaluate the effectiveness of marketing channels.

Designed for sales managers, business analysts, e-commerce teams, and decision-makers, the dashboard transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

---

# 🛠 Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 **Tableau Desktop** – Primary data visualization platform used to build interactive dashboards.
- 📂 **Tableau Data Source** – Connected and modeled transactional sales data.
- 📈 **Calculated Fields** – Used to create KPIs, AOV calculations, refund metrics, and custom business logic.
- 🔄 **Interactive Filters & Actions** – Enabled dynamic exploration across marketing channels, products, regions, and purchase dates.
- 📅 **Date Hierarchies** – Used for time-based trend analysis.
- 📁 **File Formats**
  - `.twb / .twbx` – Tableau Workbook
  - `.xlsx` – Source Dataset
  - `.png` – Dashboard Preview

# 📂 Data Source

**Source:** GameZone Sales Dataset

The dataset contains transactional sales records for GameZone products and includes information such as:

- Product Name
- Purchase Date
- Marketing Channel
- Region
- Sales Revenue
- Order Details
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Refund Amount
- Refund Count

The data was cleaned, transformed, and analyzed inside Tableau before creating the dashboard.

# ✨ Dashboard Features & Highlights

## 📌 Business Problem

GameZone sells multiple gaming products across various regions using multiple marketing channels.

Business teams needed an interactive dashboard to answer important questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which products receive the most refunds?
- Which marketing channels drive the best sales?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which regions contribute the most revenue?
- What is the average order value?
- Which products have poor refund performance?

Analyzing thousands of transaction records manually is difficult and time-consuming.

---

## 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard was designed to:

- Monitor overall business performance.
- Analyze product-wise sales trends.
- Compare sales across different regions.
- Track refund behavior by product.
- Measure business KPIs in real time.
- Support strategic decisions using interactive filters.

---

# 📊 Dashboard Walkthrough

## 🔹 Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard by:

- Marketing Channel
- Product Name
- Purchase Time
- Region

Every visualization updates automatically based on the selected filters.

---

## 🔹 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides high-level business metrics including:

- 💰 Total Sales
- 📦 Total Orders
- 💵 Average Order Value (AOV)

These KPIs provide an instant snapshot of overall business performance.

---

## 🔹 Sales Trend (Sparkline)

Small trend charts display the movement of:

- Total Sales
- Average Order Value
- Total Orders

allowing users to quickly identify growth patterns and seasonal changes.

---

## 🔹 Sales by Product

A multi-line chart compares product sales over time.

Users can:

- Identify top-performing products
- Detect declining sales
- Compare product performance throughout different periods

---

## 🔹 Sales Mix by Product

A stacked area chart visualizes the contribution of each product to total sales over time.

This helps identify:

- Revenue share
- Product contribution
- Market dominance
- Changes in product mix

---

## 🔹 Sales Summary Table

An interactive table displays product-level metrics including:

- Refunded Sales
- Average Order Value
- Refund Count

This allows quick comparison across products.

---

## 🔹 Refund Analysis

Dedicated refund visualizations help analyze:

- Refund trends by product
- Refund contribution over time
- Products generating the highest refund costs

This enables better quality control and customer satisfaction monitoring.

---

## 🔹 Regional Sales Performance

A stacked column chart displays sales across different regions.

Users can compare:

- APAC
- EMEA
- LATAM
- NA
- Other Regions

to understand geographic business performance.
